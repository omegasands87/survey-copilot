# Earnings Intelligence — Out of Scope

**Status:** Excluded from Survey Copilot v1.0 by product owner decision.

Survey Copilot v1.0 will not track:
- Survey earnings or income.
- Offered, pending, credited, or withdrawable rewards.
- Points, gift cards, balances, cash-out, or payout history.
- Time spent for hourly earnings calculations.

This document is retained only as a historical planning artifact. It is not part of the v1.0 product scope, implementation plan, navigation, database requirements, or acceptance criteria.

Any future addition requires a separate explicit product-scope decision and corresponding updates to the Product Definition and all affected specifications.
